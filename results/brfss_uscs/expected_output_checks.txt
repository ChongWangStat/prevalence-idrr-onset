=== 3. Window implied by completed-year ages
    A case told a years ago has (current age - age at diagnosis) = 0 with probability 1 - a (a < 1), and = 0 or 1 with
    probability 1 (a < 1) or 2 - a (1 <= a < 2). Without loss of cases the expected counts at differences 0, 1, 2 are 1 : 2 : 2.
    men    unweighted counts at differences 0..5: [232, 384, 390, 296, 258, 253];  0 : 1 : 2 = 1 : 1.66 : 1.68   weighted 1 : 1.96 : 1.83
    women  unweighted counts at differences 0..5: [248, 414, 396, 318, 283, 277];  0 : 1 : 2 = 1 : 1.67 : 1.60   weighted 1 : 1.70 : 1.60
    both   unweighted counts at differences 0..5: [480, 798, 786, 614, 541, 530];  0 : 1 : 2 = 1 : 1.66 : 1.64   weighted 1 : 1.82 : 1.71
    mean time since diagnosis under the two weights (no loss): 1/3 year (difference 0) and 7/9 year (difference 0 or 1)

    ROR / POR = (recent_M / cases_M) / (recent_F / cases_F): ratio of the recent fractions; the never-diagnosed cancel,
